Integrated Rate Laws

Here is the differential equation that represents the concentration vs. time of a reactant in a reaction.

$$ \frac{d[A]}{dt} = -k[A]^n $$

Let's derive the general case!

$$ \frac{1}{[A]^n}*\frac{d[A]}{dt} = -k $$

$$ \frac{1}{[A]^n}d[A] = -kdt $$

$$[A]^{-n} d[A] = -kdt$$

$$\int_{[A_0]}^{[A]}[A]^{-n} d[A] = -\int_{0}^{t}kdt$$

$$\int x^a dx = \frac{x^{a+1}}{a+1} + C\; , a \neq -1 $$

$$\int_{x_0}^{x_f} f(x) dx = F(x_f) - F(x_0) $$

$$\left[\frac{[A]^{-n+1}}{-n+1}\right]^{[A]}_{[A_0]} = -k(t-0) $$

$$\frac{[A]^{-n+1}}{-n+1} - \frac{[A_0]^{-n+1}}{-n+1} = -kt \; , n \neq 1 $$

For n = 1

$$\int_{[A_0]}^{[A]}[A]^{-1} d[A] = -\int_{0}^{t}kdt$$

$$\int_{[A_0]}^{[A]}\frac{1}{[A]} d[A] = -kt$$

$$\left[\; ln[A]\; \right]^{[A]}_{[A_0]} = -kt $$

$$ln[A] - ln[A_0] = -kt $$

$$ln\frac{[A]}{[A_0]} = -kt $$

Order Number Formula in form of y = mx + b Formula solved for
$n=0$ $[A] = -kt + [A_0] $ $[A] = -kt + [A_0] $
$n=1$ $ln[A] = -kt+ln[A_0]$ $[A] = [A_0] e^{-kt}$
$n=2$ $\frac{1}{[A]} = kt+\frac{1}{[A_0]}$ $[A] = \frac{[A_0]}{[A_0]kt+1}$
$n=3$ $\frac{1}{2[A]^2} = kt+\frac{1}{2[A_0]^2}$ $[A] = \sqrt{\frac{[A_0]^2}{2kt[A_0]^2 +1}}$
$n \neq 1$ $$\frac{[A]^{-n+1}}{-n+1} - \frac{[A_0]^{-n+1}}{-n+1} = -kt$$ $$[A] = \left( kt(n-1) + [A_0]^{-n+1} \right)^{\frac{1}{1-n}}$$
